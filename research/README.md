# Research material

Papers, thesis writing and literature analysis live here, separately from the [runnable experiments](../runtime/README.md).

- [literature/papers](literature/papers): GeoFM, survey and benchmarking papers.
- [literature/datasets](literature/datasets): papers describing datasets.
- [reports](reports): LaTeX sources, tables, figures and retained PDF exports.
- [notes](notes): research notes and paper recaps.
- [notebooks/literature](notebooks/literature): literature analysis and plotting.

Compile reports from their `sources/` directory so relative figure and table paths resolve. The literature notebook locates the checkout from its working directory and saves figures to `research/reports/preliminary/figures/`.

The paper renamer remains a utility under `runtime/scripts/`; its default destination is `research/literature/papers/`.
