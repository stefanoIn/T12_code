## What the permissions mean

- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits copying and redistribution with attribution, licence information and change notices. Retain author names and notices in the PDF, link the original paper and licence, and identify modifications. Separately excluded images or other third-party material may have different rights.
- [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/) permits non-commercial redistribution of an unchanged work with attribution. A private repository is not automatically non-commercial: the purpose of use matters.
- [arXiv's reuse guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/reuse.html) explains that its non-exclusive distribution licence does not directly grant readers permission to repost full papers. Dataset/code licences mentioned inside a paper are not automatically licences for the paper.
- [IEEE's copyright guidance](https://www.computer.org/publications/author-resources/peer-review/copyright) distinguishes personal use from uses requiring permission. The six flagged local files have institutional-access restrictions and no article-level CC grant was found.
- [NeurIPS' copyright FAQ](https://neurips.cc/FAQ/Copyright) says authors retain copyright and license NeurIPS to distribute papers. That alone does not establish your re-upload permission.
- [GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service#d-user-generated-content) require rights to post third-party content. Keeping the repository private does not supply missing rights.

Preserve each paper's own licence; do not treat third-party PDFs as covered by a blanket licence for your thesis code. The source links below identify the exact arXiv version where available. PDF-based grants were read in the local article; DOI links identify the publication. A link on an unresolved row provides provenance, not permission.

## Audit files and limits

- [CSV checklist](pdf_upload_audit.csv): one row per PDF, exact path, finding, evidence, and suggested action.
- [JSON inventory](pdf_rights_inventory.json): the same findings with SHA-256 hashes, sizes, page counts, and Git tracking status at audit time. It stores licence summaries rather than extracted paper text.
- All 58 project PDFs were readable for text extraction. Some PDFs emitted rendering-resource warnings; this is not a pixel-by-pixel inspection or a complete audit of embedded third-party artwork.
